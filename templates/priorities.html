Priority Rules

How Priority Scoring Works:

Each news article is scored against these keyword rules. When an article's title or summary contains a keyword, the corresponding score is applied. The highest matching score determines the article's priority level. Hacking incidents always receive top (critical) priority by default.

Automated Analysis: Every day at midnight, the system analyzes recent news patterns and adjusts scores automatically. Frequently matched keywords get boosted; unused keywords get lowered. New keywords discovered in hacking incidents are added automatically. You can review and override any changes.
